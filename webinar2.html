About This Session

Your code depends on code
you've never reviewed

Most modern applications are 80% third-party code — libraries, packages, and transitive dependencies your team didn't write and may never have reviewed. That's the new attack surface.

SolarWinds, Log4j, and the recent Trivy and Checkmarx GitHub Actions compromises didn't touch a single line of custom code. They compromised what your code depends on.

This session is for AppSec and security engineers who are done scanning and ready to actually manage supply chain risk — with tools, frameworks, and a response playbook they can use the same week.

What You'll Learn
  • Turn your SBOM into an incident response tool Not just a compliance document — make it operational so it actually helps when something breaks.
  • Harden your CI/CD pipeline beyond the dependency scanner The step most teams miss — and attackers know it.
  • Vet open-source libraries before they reach your codebase A repeatable three-step process your team can run every time.
  • Execute the three immediate actions when a dependency is compromised Isolate, freeze, rotate — in the right order, without panic.
