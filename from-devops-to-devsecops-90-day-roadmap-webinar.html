About This Session

A clear path from
DevOps to DevSecOps.

Making the shift from DevOps to DevSecOps is one of the most impactful changes an engineering organization can make — yet most teams struggle to know where to start, what to prioritize, and how to demonstrate measurable progress along the way. Security can't be bolted on at the end. It has to be woven into every stage of the delivery pipeline.

This webinar presents a proven, structured 90-day transformation roadmap that gives teams a concrete path from where they are today to a mature, security-integrated delivery pipeline. Drawing on hands-on experience building DevSecOps programs across diverse organizations, Igor Matlin walks through the frameworks, tooling decisions, and culture shifts that actually move the needle — without slowing your teams down.

What You'll Learn
  • The key differences between DevOps and DevSecOps — and what true security integration looks like
  • A phase-by-phase breakdown of the 90-day transformation framework — what to do in Days 1–30, 31–60, and 61–90
  • The tools, processes, and culture shifts that matter most at each stage of the journey
  • How to measure and communicate security maturity to leadership and stakeholders
