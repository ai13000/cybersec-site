About This Session

80% of your app is code
you didn't write

Most modern applications are 80% third-party code — libraries, packages, and transitive dependencies your team didn't write and may never have reviewed. That's the new attack surface.

SolarWinds, Log4j, and the recent Trivy and Checkmarx GitHub Actions compromises didn't touch a single line of custom code. They compromised what your code depends on.

This session is for AppSec and security engineers who are done scanning and ready to actually manage supply chain risk — with tools, frameworks, and a response playbook they can use the same week.

What You'll Learn

Walk away with a playbook,
not just slides

  • Turn your SBOM into an incident response tool Not just a compliance document — make it operational.
  • Harden your CI/CD pipeline beyond the dependency scanner The hardening step most teams overlook entirely.
  • Vet open-source libraries before they reach your codebase A repeatable three-step process you can apply immediately.
  • Execute the three immediate actions when a dependency is compromised Isolate, freeze, rotate — in the right order.
