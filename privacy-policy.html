Section 1

Who We Are

CyberSec Gard provides DevSecOps consulting, security assessments, and training services to organizations in the United States and European Union. We operate the website at cybersecgard.com and associated landing pages.

For the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), CyberSec Gard acts as the data controller for personal information collected through our website and webinar registration forms. Questions about this policy may be directed to info@cybersecgard.com.

Section 2

Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you provide directly to us when registering for a webinar or contacting us through our website. This may include:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Organization or company name
  • Job title
  • Phone number (where provided)

We also collect limited technical data automatically through Google Analytics when you visit our website — see Section 6 for details.

We do not collect payment information, government identifiers, or any special categories of personal data under GDPR Article 9.

Section 3

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Webinar registration & delivery — to confirm your registration, send event reminders, and provide access details.
  • Post-event follow-up — to share recordings, slides, and resources related to the event you attended.
  • Marketing communications — to inform you of upcoming webinars, reports, and services. You may opt out at any time.
  • Service enquiries — to respond to questions submitted through our contact forms.
  • Analytics & improvement — to understand how our website is used and improve our content and services.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Section 4  ·  GDPR

Legal Basis for Processing

For individuals in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom, we process personal data on the following legal bases under GDPR Article 6:

Consent
Sending marketing emails and newsletters. You may withdraw consent at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email or by contacting us directly.
Legitimate Interests
Processing registrations, responding to service enquiries, and post-event follow-ups directly related to an event you registered for. We have assessed that these interests are not overridden by your rights and freedoms.
Contract
Where we have a service agreement in place, processing necessary to deliver that service.
Section 5

Third-Party Services

We work with the following third-party services that may process your personal information on our behalf. Each acts as a data processor under our instruction.

HubSpot, Inc.
We use HubSpot to manage webinar registration forms and contact records. HubSpot is headquartered in the United States and is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. For more information, see HubSpot's Privacy Policy.
Google LLC
We use Google Analytics 4 to analyze website traffic. Data collected is anonymized and aggregated. Google LLC is headquartered in the United States and participates in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. See Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not share your personal information with any other third parties except as required by law.

Section 6

Cookies & Analytics

Our website uses cookies — small text files stored in your browser — through Google Analytics to collect information about how visitors use our site. The data collected is aggregated and does not identify you personally.

Google Analytics cookies collect information such as the pages visited, time spent on the site, and general geographic region. This data helps us understand what content is most useful and where to improve.

Managing cookies: You can disable Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. You may also configure your browser to refuse or delete cookies at any time; doing so will not affect your ability to use our website.

We do not use advertising, targeting, or social media tracking cookies.

Section 7  ·  GDPR

International Data Transfers

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or United Kingdom, your personal data may be transferred to and processed in the United States, where our service providers HubSpot and Google are based.

These transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards:

  • Both HubSpot and Google LLC are certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, which provides an adequacy mechanism recognized by the European Commission.
  • Where required, transfers are also covered by Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission.

You may request a copy of the safeguards in place for any transfer by contacting us at info@cybersecgard.com.

Section 8

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

Webinar registrants
Up to 3 years from the event date, unless you request earlier deletion or unsubscribe from all communications.
Contact enquiries
Up to 2 years from the date of last contact.
Analytics data
Retained in Google Analytics for 14 months (aggregated, non-personal).

When your data is no longer required, it is securely deleted or anonymized.

Section 9

Your Rights

If you are in the EEA or UK (GDPR), you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Right of Access
Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
Right to Rectification
Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
Right to Erasure
Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
Right to Restriction
Request that we limit how we process your data in certain circumstances.
Right to Portability
Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
Right to Object
Object to processing based on legitimate interests, including direct marketing.
Withdraw Consent
Withdraw consent for marketing at any time without affecting prior processing.
Lodge a Complaint
File a complaint with your local data protection authority if you believe your rights have been violated.

If you are a California resident (CCPA), you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect, use, disclose, and sell (we do not sell personal information).
  • Delete personal information we have collected from you, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. CyberSec Gard does not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Non-discrimination — we will not penalize you for exercising any of these rights.

To exercise any of the rights above, please contact us using the details in Section 10. We will respond to verifiable requests within 30 days (GDPR) or 45 days (CCPA).
