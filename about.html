Our Methodology

A process built on
adversarial thinking.

01

Reconnaissance Mapping

We map your external attack surface before adversaries do — domains, exposed services, credential leaks, supplier risk.

02

Attack Surface Analysis

Deep technical enumeration of your application portfolio, infrastructure, and data flows. Every trust boundary documented.

03

Threat Modeling

Structured adversarial analysis using STRIDE and PASTA. Risk-ranked findings tied to business impact, not CVSS scores in isolation.

04

Remediation Architecture

We don't just hand you a list. We design the fixes — security controls, code-level guidance, infrastructure changes — integrated into your sprint cycles.

05

Continuous Monitoring

Security is not a project. We establish detection pipelines, alerting logic, and quarterly review cadences — so you see threats before they escalate.
